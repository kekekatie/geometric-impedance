## The result

**In the pristine bulk the two substrates are nearly equivalent** — 0.9996 against
0.9816, a gap of 0.018. This is the boundary-sensitivity finding restated: with
boundary contamination removed, Penrose addresses its own privileged sites almost
as well as AB does.

**Under phason disorder they separate immediately and sharply.** At the first
disorder step, amplitude 0.05:

- AB loses 0.0067
- Penrose loses 0.1291

Penrose loses roughly nineteen times as much address readability from the same
perturbation. The gap opens from 0.018 to 0.140 in one step, reaches ~0.21 by
amplitude 0.20, and then plateaus.

## What this means for the exo/endo claim

The distinction is real, but it is a claim about **fragility, not presence**.

Both substrates possess a perpendicular-space address channel, and in clean bulk
both are close to perfect. What differs is how that channel survives
perturbation. AB degrades gracefully across the whole disorder range, retaining
0.84 at amplitude 0.40. Penrose collapses at the first sign of strain.

This is consistent with the boundary result rather than in tension with it: both
boundary truncation and phason disorder are perturbations of the acceptance
condition, and Penrose is markedly more sensitive to both. The unified statement
is that **Penrose's address channel is intrinsically more fragile than
Ammann-Beenker's**, and that the published contrast at interior-75% conflated
this fragility with absence.

That is a narrower claim than the papers make, and a better one — resilience
under strain is a more physically meaningful property than readability on a
pristine patch, and it is the version that survives every control applied so far:
nonlinear model, matched positive rate, matched scale, bulk crop, shuffle null,
and replication.

## Remaining checks

- One label definition. The matched-rate composite should be cross-checked
  against at least one independent privileged-site definition.
- 4 seeds per point; the trend is far larger than the error bars, but 10 would be
  standard.
- Disorder amplitude is in perpendicular-space units specific to these
  generators, so it is not directly comparable to any physical phason strain
  measure without a conversion.
- Why Penrose is more fragile remains unmeasured. Its acceptance region is four
  pentagons indexed by lift sum against AB's single octagon, which is the obvious
  place to look, but that is a hypothesis.
