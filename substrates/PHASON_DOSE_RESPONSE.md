# Address channel under phason disorder: Ammann-Beenker vs Penrose

Both substrates generated by cut-and-project at matched scale (~16,800 and
~17,500 vertices), perturbed by independent jitter of each candidate's
perpendicular coordinate before the acceptance test, scored identically, 4
disorder seeds per point. A shuffle null runs beside every measurement.

```
python3 phason_dose_response.py --seeds 4 --ab-extent 32 --pen-extent 26
```

## What the curves say

**AB degrades gracefully and never collapses.** From 0.9997 to 0.8356 across the
full disorder range. Even with 40% jitter on every perpendicular coordinate, and
edge Jaccard well below half, the address channel remains strongly readable.

**Penrose starts weak and stays weak.** Roughly flat within error from 0.05 to
0.20 (0.72, 0.76, 0.75), then declining to 0.62.

**The gap is approximately constant.** Between +0.19 and +0.26 at every disorder
level. AB does not converge toward Penrose under strain; the two curves run
close to parallel. The exo/endo separation is therefore not a property of the
pristine substrates only — it survives across the whole perturbation range
tested.

## Correction to an earlier speculation

The single-seed run at ~5,400 vertices suggested that AB under strong disorder
(0.748) landed near unperturbed Penrose (0.786), and that note called the
equivalence "a well-posed question". At proper scale with replication it does not
hold: AB at disorder 0.40 is 0.8356 ± 0.0104, comfortably above Penrose's best
value anywhere in the sweep (0.7600 ± 0.0269). The apparent coincidence was
small-sample noise. Strained AB is not Penrose at rest.

## The limitation that matters most

Penrose's shared-core label is intrinsically sparse: 42–103 positives from
~13,000 interior vertices, against AB's 242–653. That is why the Penrose error
bars are three to six times wider, and why the zero-disorder point could not be
measured at all despite a 17,500-vertex patch.

This is a property of the label definition, not of the substrate. The label is
the intersection of three top-quartile sets, and on Penrose that intersection is
nearly empty. Before this comparison can carry real weight, the label needs
redefining so that both substrates yield comparable positive rates — a fixed
positive fraction per substrate would be the obvious approach, and would also
remove positive-rate as a difference between the two arms.
