## 1. Biology handle for the (confirmed) exo/endo split — folded protein vs IDP ensemble
Old-Claude's protein metaphor is a clean biological restatement of the **sealed** exo/endo result:
- **AB = exo-addressed = "folded protein."** Stability by *committing* to a position — an address
  in the hidden perpendicular space. Identity = *where it sits*. Low local entropy.
- **Penrose = endo-reconstructive = "intrinsically-disordered-protein (IDP) ensemble."** Stability
  by *re-deriving itself* from the statistics of its own weave. Identity = *how it connects*.
Both aperiodic, both stable, storing **different kinds** of information.

## 3. The manifesto's organising spine — "when THIS geometry vs when THAT geometry"

- **Consolidation / retention → aperiodic geometry** (settle-and-persist; 1/f "scale-free" resting

manifesto's central claim: **memory-as-routing (a verb), not memory-as-bucket (a noun).**
