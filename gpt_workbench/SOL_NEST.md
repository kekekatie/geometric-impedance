## Scientific distinctions to preserve

- **Spectral structure is not literal transport.** A per-vertex LDOS or other spectral result can
  establish structured spectral organization without establishing wavepacket motion, conduction,
  or a same-band dynamical mechanism. Transport language requires a genuinely dynamical endpoint
  whose launch, window, boundary handling, controls, and uncertainty rule survive review.
- **Compression, representation, and irreducibility are different questions.** If a finite-radius
  address-free physical representation absorbs an address increment, that may indicate physical
  compression. If the increment changes when representational capacity or pipeline parity is
  matched, that may indicate a representation effect. Only after strong physical descriptions,
  parity controls, finite-size checks, and conditional nulls fail to remove a stable increment is
  an irreducibility claim in view. These outcomes must not be collapsed into one binary verdict.
- The perpendicular-space address is an analysis representation, not automatically an additional
  physical degree of freedom. Selection rules and controls can remain address-correlated even
  when they do not explicitly use address, so admitted populations must be reported and tested.
