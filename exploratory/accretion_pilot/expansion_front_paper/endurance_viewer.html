Definitions and limits

Stars are decorated tile arrangements at frontier vertices, plus complete stars within two graph edges of the frontier. Clusters contain 2–4 connected true-frontier edges and their supporting tiles. Classes preserve decoration and scale, allowing translation and rotation; mirror partners have separate conventional +/− labels, or “achiral”. Coordinates are numerically canonicalized, not symbolically exact.

A link requires a newly appearing daughter containing a tile added in this step that touches the parent. Direct activity means an attachment at a parent frontier edge. Nearby activity means attachment touching a pre-step vertex within two graph edges. These counters belong to each fixed occurrence, not a proved enduring object. Leaving the observation band, losing a pattern, closure, and final removal are separate recorded events. Initial occurrences have unknown earlier lifetimes. A jam or the final step ends observation; it is not dissolution.

Only physical-space geometry is analysed. No perpendicular-space, particle, or spin measurement is made. Candidate transitions overlap heavily and are not independent decay channels. The original scheduler still coordinates the whole frontier.
