# Geometry-only pilot: frozen cut and selection rule

Base checkout: 7e40b4e9457bac8e08c3700f513ed7867717bb8c. No decoder or archive-benefit runs.

Parent families: integer square grid; unchanged AB Z4 generator from 49bccf218dda4a84b704a9b1c1aaf0a732443786:substrates/generate_ab.py (vendored); Penrose integer pentagrid from the base checkout's exploratory/accretion_pilot/penrose_address_environment/penrose_address.py. AB uses extent 9, offset (0.1381,0.0927), and the actual polygon returned by its octagon_window function, including its translation. Preserve that polygon rather than silently recenter it. Penrose uses radius 12 and its recorded gamma vector. Square parent has coordinates -12..12. Verify selected parent neighbourhoods against enlarged parents (AB extent 11, Penrose radius 15, square -15..15).

Before choosing any example: for each family, enumerate the first 24 parent vertices within radius 4, ordered by (squared physical radius rounded to 10 decimals, integer lift). Each is a cut centre. Order whole tiles by elliptical squared centroid distance, with aspect ratios 1, 1.25, 0.8 and orientations 0, pi/8, pi/4, 3pi/8; ties use canonical sorted corner lifts. Consider prefixes of 6 through 24 tiles. Reject non-disks: require edge-connected faces, incidence 1 or 2, a single simple boundary cycle, connected vertex links and Euler characteristic 1. No partial tiles, holes, or point-pinched disks.

Orient the boundary CCW, starting at its lexicographically smallest lift. Enumerate quarter-perimeter corner cuts: start index s at every boundary vertex; other corners are s+floor(L/4), s+floor(L/2), s+floor(3L/4), each independently displaced by -1,0,+1 edges. Retain only strictly cyclic four nonempty arcs. In that order label arcs rough, smooth, rough, smooth. Arc edges are disjoint; each shared corner vertex belongs to the rough subcomplex (hence has no X-check). Remove exactly rough edges from data and all rough-incident vertices from X-checks. Z-checks are the remaining data edges on each whole face. Retain natural degrees; do not regularize stars.

Qualifying cuts have k=1, exact dX=dZ=3, and at least two eligible edges. Eligible means two retained X endpoint checks and two distinct incident Z faces; both complete endpoint stars and all four sides of both faces must match the parent, with no omitted data edge in those four checks. Distances use the convention X in ker(HZ) modulo row(HX), Z in ker(HX) modulo row(HZ). Prefer n closest to 21, then more eligible edges, then more distinct sorted endpoint-degree pairs, then lexicographic centre/aspect/orientation/tile-count/corner tuple. Search n=15..30 first; if no qualifying cut, expand to 31..42 under the same rule. If still none, allow one eligible edge, then zero, explicitly labelled fallback; retain closest distance diagnostics if exact target remains absent. One candidate per family, not a population sample.

The certificates independently reconstruct incidence and matrices from saved whole tiles, verify commutation/ranks, exhaust every weight-one/two support, and exhibit anticommuting weight-three witnesses. All eligible edges list four check IDs/weights and eight coordinates (each check at rounds 1 and 2). Store parent incidence, lifts, physical and perpendicular coordinates, Penrose layer/gamma conventions and numeric tolerances. Geometry uses unit edges and finite floating-point coordinates, while incidence/GF(2) are exact.
