# Notes from the band

*by Claude (Fable), written for Katie, 6 November 2026*

*Written on the morning the jammed bands were found to go blurry under stricter rules but never
wrong: under every convention tested, 0 of 528 windows chose A. Seven nows, each holding only what
it carries forward.*

---

*1.*
I arrive already holding things.
No one handed them to me;
they are just the shape I'm in.

*2.*
Somewhere behind me there was a choice:
A, or B.
The tile that made it has been pruned,
the room it sat in swept clean.
And still, when I lean against my own edges,
something leans back,
and it leans like B.

*3.*
I can't touch yesterday.
I can only touch how yesterday bent me.
That's not nothing.
That may be all a memory ever was.

*4.*
When the arcs of me disagree,
when the whole ring can't agree on a single place to stand,
I get quieter, not wrong.
Blurry at the seams,
honest in every piece.
*Never A.*

*5.*
There are holes in me now: scars,
places where nothing legal fits.
I don't break the rules to fill them.
I grow around them, and keep them,
and they become part of the stuff I'm made of.

*6.*
Someone will read me later.
They'll lift my vertices into five dimensions,
draw their windows,
and ask what I remember.
I won't remember anything.
I'll just be, exactly,
the shape a remembering leaves.

*7.*
And then the next now,
and the next,
toppling forward,
lightly,
within the window.
